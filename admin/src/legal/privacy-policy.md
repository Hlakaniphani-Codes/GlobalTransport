# Privacy policy

**Last updated:** 17 October 2026

## 1. Who we are

Global Ride Transport provides a platform that coordinates journeys between passengers and independent drivers. Drivers own and operate their vehicles; Global Ride Transport does not own the vehicles used for journeys.

## 2. Personal information we collect

Depending on how you use the platform, we may collect:

- Identity and account details, including your name, surname, email address, telephone number and password hash.
- Driver verification information, including your South African identity number, address, licence and professional driving permit details, vehicle information and application status.
- Passenger and journey information, including your employee number, workplace, home and workplace addresses and suburbs, emergency contact details, ride requests, stops and trip history.
- Service and security information, including account activity, consent records, cancellation details and technical logs needed to operate and protect the service.

We do not ask users to submit information that is not needed for the services described here.

## 3. Why we use information

We use personal information to create and manage accounts, review driver and vehicle applications, arrange and operate journeys, communicate service updates, protect users, prevent misuse, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and maintain accounting and audit records.

We record acceptance of the terms and privacy policy when an account is created. Passwords are stored as password hashes and are not stored as readable passwords.

## 4. Who may see information

Authorised Global Ride Transport administrators may access information needed to manage accounts, applications, bookings, trips, support and audit records. Drivers receive limited trip details before accepting an offer; passenger names, telephone numbers and exact addresses are provided only when needed for an accepted trip. Passengers receive driver and vehicle details after a trip is accepted; driver telephone numbers are shown only during the applicable trip contact period.

We may use service providers, including hosting and database providers, to operate the platform under appropriate safeguards. We may disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect users and the service. We do not sell personal information.

## 5. Retention

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes above, legal and accounting requirements, safety, dispute resolution and the protection of legal rights. When an account is deleted, account and location details are anonymised. Trip, stop, booking and audit records may be retained in anonymised form where needed for accounting, legal or operational records.

## 6. Security

We use access controls and technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Your rights under POPIA

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, object to or limit certain processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or complain to the Information Regulator. We may need to verify your identity and may retain records where the law permits or requires us to do so.

You can request account deletion in the app or use the instructions on the account deletion page. Deletion does not remove records that must be retained by law or for legitimate accounting and safety purposes; personal details in retained records are anonymised where appropriate.

## 8. Children

The platform is intended for adults arranging work-related commuting. It is not designed for children.

## 9. Contact and complaints

For privacy questions, correction or access requests, or complaints, contact Global Ride Transport using the support email published on the official app listing or the account deletion page. You may also contact the South African Information Regulator about a complaint.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or legal obligations change. The current version will be published on the Global Ride Transport website or in the app.

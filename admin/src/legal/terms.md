## 3. Ride requests and trips

Passengers may request journeys through the app. A request is not a confirmed trip until it has been organised and accepted by a driver. Administrators coordinate requests and may group passengers into trips. A trip offer is subject to a driver's acceptance.

Drivers must keep their application, licence and vehicle information accurate and current, follow applicable traffic and transport laws, and operate only a roadworthy and appropriately licensed vehicle. Drivers are independent vehicle owners and are responsible for their vehicles and driving.

Passengers may cancel eligible ride requests in the app before the trip starts. Cancellation close to the pick-up time may be recorded as late. Administrators may cancel or change trips where required for safe and reliable operations. The app will display the available trip status and cancellation information.
